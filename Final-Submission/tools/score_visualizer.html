Score = 100·(Tnom/T − F/Fnom − 2·D/Dmax + 0.5). With x = 1/T it is linear in (x, F, D), so the best speed is where the highest score plane just touches the reachable curve; switch the x axis to 1/T to see the plane. Trip and model tabs: point mass, no gravity, constant-acceleration burn to the cruise speed, then coast. No braking is modelled because the win is any pass within the win radius of the target.
